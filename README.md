# CastSheet

Character reference sheets for AI production: describe a character, pick a style,
and get a three-view sheet (front · side · back) plus a 2×2 expression grid,
generated by the [Pollinations](https://gen.pollinations.ai/docs) image API.
A shared seed and description keep the boards consistent, so they drop straight
into video-model reference workflows.

**Sign in with Pollinations** (Connect User Wallets): the preferred path — one click,
the app receives a scoped key for your session and you revoke it anytime from your
dashboard. **Or bring your own key (BYOP):** paste your free Pollinations key
(`sk_…` from [enter.pollinations.ai/keys](https://enter.pollinations.ai/keys)); it is
held in your browser tab only and sent solely to `gen.pollinations.ai`.

No secrets are embedded in this app: the `pk_` app key is a publishable client_id
that identifies the app on the consent screen; user-authorized `sk_` keys live in
the user's browser session.

## Use

Open the app, sign in (or add your key), describe your character, pick a style,
hit **Generate boards**. URL params prefill a fixed sheet you can share privately:

```
https://fi8vni13.github.io/castsheet/?desc=your+character&seed=12345
```
